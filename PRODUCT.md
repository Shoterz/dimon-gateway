# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Hackathon builders, teammates, and judges testing whether a local language model can safely turn natural-language banking intentions into bounded synthetic ledger actions. The primary task is to type an ordinary transfer request, inspect the deterministic safety boundary, and see the resulting SQLite state change or non-mutating refusal.

## Product Purpose

Dimon Live is an inspectable local banking-agent prototype. It demonstrates that Qwen may propose structured intent while server-owned code resolves identities, validates evidence, issues immutable receipts, and performs exactly-once synthetic ledger mutations. Success feels immediate, understandable, and credible without implying access to real payment rails.

## Brand Personality

Precise, assured, and technically transparent. The interface should feel like a live settlement console: impressive because the evidence is visible, not because safety details are hidden.

## Anti-references

Avoid consumer-banking gloss, chatbot bubbles, generic SaaS dashboards, decorative fintech gradients, and interfaces that conceal approval coordinates or imply a real PayNow connection. Avoid turning deterministic safety decisions into conversational model output.

## Design Principles

1. Show the handoff from probabilistic interpretation to deterministic execution.
2. Make successful settlement feel immediate while making ambiguity visibly non-mutating.
3. Keep the exact locked facts beside every confirmation action.
4. Treat synthetic/demo boundaries as permanent context, not fine print.
5. Prefer inspectable database evidence over decorative claims of intelligence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard-first operation, visible focus states, semantic live regions, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion behavior. Never encode success, warning, or failure through color alone.
